# Construction change-order template

Record changed scope, price and a decision before carrying out additional work.

## Copy this outline

- Project / site: ____________________
- Original quote reference: ____________________
- Change number and date: ____________________
- Reason for change: ____________________
- Added or removed scope: ____________________
- Materials, labour and price adjustment: ____________________
- Tax and revised amount: ____________________
- Expected schedule impact and assumptions: ____________________
- Decision, approver and decision date: ____________________

## Use it with the job

- Keep the original scope and change as separate records.
- Share the changed scope and price for online approval.
- If a captured signature is required, use your separate signing process.
- After approval, invoice the agreed work and review phase dates and material ordering.
